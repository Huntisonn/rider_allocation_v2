## Aptitude & Logical Reasoning

### Number & Arithmetic
1. A train 150m long passes a pole in 15 seconds. What is the speed? *(10 m/s)*
2. What is 15% of 240? *(36)*
3. If 6 men can do a job in 12 days, how many days for 9 men? *(8 days)*
4. A number when divided by 3 gives remainder 2, when divided by 5 gives remainder 3. Find the smallest such number. *(8)*
5. What is the sum of the first 50 natural numbers? *(1275)*
6. A box has 4 red, 5 blue, 6 green balls. Probability of picking a red ball? *(4/15)*
7. A shopkeeper sells at 20% profit. If CP is ₹500, what is SP? *(₹600)*
8. Two pipes A and B fill a tank in 20 and 30 minutes. Together, how long? *(12 minutes)*
9. The ratio of ages of A and B is 3:4. After 5 years, ratio becomes 7:9. Find current ages. *(A=15, B=20)*
10. If log 2 = 0.301, find log 8. *(0.903)*

## 📝 Quick Tips for Interview Success

| Tip | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **STAR Method** | Use Situation, Task, Action, Result for behavioral answers |
| **Think Aloud** | Verbalize your thought process during technical problems |
| **Clarify First** | Ask clarifying questions before solving any problem |
| **Time/Space Complexity** | Always analyze and mention Big-O for DSA solutions |
| **Edge Cases** | Consider null, empty, negative, and boundary inputs |
| **Practice Daily** | Solve at least 1-2 problems on LeetCode/HackerRank |
| **Mock Interviews** | Practice with peers or on Pramp/Interviewing.io |
| **Be Honest** | If you don't know, say so and explain how you'd find out |
| **Research Company** | Know the company's products, culture, and recent news |
